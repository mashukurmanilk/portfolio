About Me

Academic & Engineering Journey

I am an undergraduate student studying Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), one of Bangladesh's premier engineering institutions. My academic background has immersed me in rigorous computer science foundations: data structures, algorithm design, operating systems, and discrete mathematics.

Driven by hands-on engineering, I actively build full-scale web platforms while training and deploying Machine Learning & Deep Learning models. I love translating theoretical linear algebra and calculus concepts into trained neural networks that solve practical real-world challenges.

I specialize in two complementary domains: modern full-stack web architecture (Next.js 14, React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB) and applied machine learning & deep learning (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-Learn).
